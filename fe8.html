Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones - Army Randomizer

Fire Emblem: The Sacred Stones' final chapter allows you to bring in 2 required units and 10 units of your choosing,
up to a maximum of 12 total units.

This randomizer randomly selects these 10 units based on your choice of route and other preferences you may have.
Note:
There are two pairs of units that are recruitable based on decisions that are less player-oriented,
so they are paired together regardless of who you are able to recruit.

These two pairs of units are Wallace / Geitz & Karel / Harken.

Route Selection
This option chooses whose route you will follow based on player decision or randomization.

Unpromoted Restriction

This option selects units that begin in a class that can change class using an appropriate promotion item, and excludes units like Marcus who begin as a promoted Paladin class.

*Tethys is considered an unpromoted unit for this purpose.



Ban 3 Units

These options will remove the following unit(s) from the randomization pool, based on your preferences and for your convenience.
